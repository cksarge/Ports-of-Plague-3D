Ports of Plague is a free, educational 3D board game about the Black Death (1347-1353), made by high-school students for a history class and built with Unreal Engine 5.

We would like to show the Unreal Engine splash-screen logo when the game opens, to credit the engine. We use the official logo exactly as supplied by Epic, white on black, with no changes to its shape or colour. It is shown alone, centred, at half the height of the screen, with the required trademark notice beneath it. It fades in, is held for about two seconds, and fades out.
